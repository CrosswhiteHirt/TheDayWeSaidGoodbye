A goodbye worth rewriting

What if memory were your only way out?

Years after a farewell that changed everything, Emily is pulled back into the same summer day. Noah, Sophie, and Daniel each hold a different piece of the truth. Every conversation changes who trusts her. Every discovery brings midnight closer.

Choose what to investigate, decide which memory survives the reset, and shape a final rescue with consequences that reach all the way into the future.
